Each letter is one small text file, named like this:   vol2-letter07.txt
  vol2     = volume number (1 to 5)
  letter07 = letter number (01 to 20), always two digits

Inside the file:
  title: Open when you miss me
  from: Om, Jinu          <- optional. Names separated by commas. Shows as "Om & Jinu".
  card: pink              <- pink, purple or white
  stickers: ❤️ 🌸          <- optional
  ---
  The letter text goes here.

Easiest way to make one: open write.html on your site and fill the form.
